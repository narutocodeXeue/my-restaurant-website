01

More Than
a Cafe.

Pablo is a room built around an idea — that a table can be a canvas, and an evening can be a composition.

Food, drink, art and conversation share the same space here. Plates are considered. Pours are deliberate. Walls keep the dialogue going long after the plates are cleared. Light falls where it should.

It is a cafe by day, a lounge by night, and a gallery in between — shaped by colour, line and form, and by the people who fill it.

04 — Art

An Experience Inspired by Picasso. line, form & colour

Not a tribute act — an atmosphere. The idea that a space can be composed the way a painting is: with weight, balance, interruption and contrast.

You'll find it in the walls, the light, the plating, the pour, and the way an evening at Pablo unfolds. Original artistic direction throughout — nothing copied, everything considered.
